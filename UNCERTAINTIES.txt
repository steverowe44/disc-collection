UNCERTAINTIES — OPEN ITEMS
==========================

Every field that went into collection.csv as a guess rather than a fact gets logged here,
with the image number and enough physical detail to find the disc on the shelf.

  [ ] = open, needs Stephen's eyes
  [x] = resolved (moved to the RESOLVED section, kept for history — never deleted)

Each item has a stable ID (U001, U002...) so commits can reference it:
  "Resolve U004: Thief is StudioCanal, not Arrow"

Work through them at any pace. Answering one means: correct the row in collection.csv,
tick the item, move it to RESOLVED with the answer, commit.
